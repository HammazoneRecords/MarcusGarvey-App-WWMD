The provided sources do not contain direct quotes from Marcus Garvey specifically stating his views on unity. However, they describe his fundamental goals and the program of the organization he founded as centered on unity:

*   His goal was to unite the African Diaspora so they could work together to rebuild their great nation [Source: marcus_garvey_teachers_notes, Locator: pdf:page:0003].
*   He founded the "Universal Negro Improvement Association and African Communities (Imperial) League... with the program of uniting the Negro peoples of the world into one great body to establish a country and government absolutely their own" [Source: marcus_garvey_selected_writings, Locator: pdf:page:0010].
