## Overview

Whirlwind KB ("the app") is a source-grounded knowledge base and decision-support tool. This policy describes what data we collect and how it is used.

## Data we collect and store

- **On your device only (localStorage):** The app may store preferences such as theme (light/dark), saved fact bookmarks, toolkit edits, Garvey Lens results and selected action steps, and optional API configuration (e.g. Ollama URL, API keys for Open Router/OpenAI/Gemini). This data never leaves your device unless you use features that call external services (e.g. Garvey Lens, which sends your situation text to our backend and/or the AI provider you configure).
- **On our servers (if you use the hosted API):** When you use the Garvey Lens or chat, we may process your input (situation or query) on our backend to generate responses. Session or usage data may be stored in server logs or databases for operational and safety purposes. Check with your deployment owner (e.g. Mindwave Jamaica) for how long this data is retained.
- **We do not sell your data.** We do not use your data for advertising or third-party marketing.

## Third-party services

- **Google Gemini (or other LLM):** If the app uses Google Gemini or another AI provider, that provider’s privacy policy and terms apply to the data sent to them. Our backend may send your query/situation to the provider to generate responses.
- **Fonts (e.g. Google Fonts):** The app may load fonts from Google; see Google’s privacy policy for how they handle requests.

## Your choices

- You can clear app data by clearing your browser’s storage for the app’s origin (e.g. site settings → clear data). That will remove saved facts, toolkit edits, and API settings stored locally.
- If the app is offered with an optional account in the future, you may have additional choices (e.g. delete account, export data); those will be described in the product and in an updated policy.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
