## Getting Started

### First Launch
When you open **Whirlwind KB**, you'll land on the **Home** page featuring a daily reflection. The navigation bar at the bottom provides access to all sections:

| Icon | Section | Purpose |
|:----:|:--------|:--------|
| 🏠 | Home | Daily quotes and inspiration |
| 📚 | Knowledge Base | Searchable historical facts |
| 🧭 | WWMD | AI-powered decision assistant |
| 🛠️ | Toolkit | Organization templates |
| 👤 | Profile | Saved items, theme, AI/API settings |

### Theme Toggle
Look for the **sun/moon icon** in the top corner to switch between **Light Mode** (warm parchment tones) and **Dark Mode** (rich charcoal palette).

## Home — Daily Reflection

The Home page displays a curated piece of wisdom each day.

### What You'll See
- **Quote of the Day**: A powerful excerpt from Marcus Garvey's speeches or writings
- **Context Panel**: Historical background explaining when and why this was said
- **Source Citation**: The exact document, speech, or publication (the "Receipt")

### How to Use It
1. **Read slowly** — Let the words sink in
2. **Tap the source** — View full citation details
3. **Save for later** — Tap the bookmark icon to add to your Profile
4. **Share** — Use the share button to spread the wisdom

## Knowledge Base — Explore the Archive

A comprehensive database of verified historical facts, each backed by primary sources.

### Browsing Facts
- **Scroll** through the curated collection
- **Filter by Category**: Politics, Economics, Self-Reliance, Education, Pan-Africanism
- **Search**: Type keywords to find specific topics

### Understanding Fact Cards
Each card displays:
- **Headline**: The key claim or statement
- **Summary**: Brief explanation
- **Receipt Badge**: Number of supporting citations

### Viewing Details
Tap any fact card to see:
- Full historical context
- All supporting citations with direct links
- Related facts you might explore
- Option to save to your collection

### The "Receipts" System
Every fact has verifiable citations:
- 📖 **Book**: Published works and biographies
- 📰 **Article**: Newspaper and magazine pieces
- 🎤 **Speech**: Recorded addresses and lectures
- 📜 **Document**: Official records and correspondence

## WWMD — The Garvey Lens

**"What Would Marcus Do?"** — Your AI-powered decision assistant.

### How It Works
1. **Ask a Question**: Type any situation, dilemma, or decision you're facing
2. **Receive Guidance**: The AI returns principle-based counsel rooted in Garvey's philosophy
3. **Review Sources**: Every response includes citations from actual texts

### Example Questions
- *"How should I approach starting a business in my community?"*
- *"What would Marcus say about the importance of education?"*
- *"How do I stay motivated when facing discrimination?"*
- *"What principles should guide a new organization?"*

### Reading Responses
Each WWMD response contains:
- **Direct Answer**: Practical guidance for your situation
- **Historical Analog**: A similar challenge from Garvey's life/era
- **Principle Applied**: The underlying philosophy being referenced
- **Citations**: Exact sources supporting the response

### Important Note
> 🎯 **Source-Grounded, Not Impersonation**
> 
> The Garvey Lens draws from verified historical texts. It doesn't pretend to "be" Marcus Garvey—it applies his documented principles to modern situations.

## Toolkit — Organization Building

Practical templates for community organizing and institution-building.

### Available Templates
| Template | Purpose |
|:---------|:--------|
| Chapter Constitution | Foundational bylaws for new organizations |
| Meeting Agenda | Structured format for productive gatherings |
| Membership Drive | Campaign planning worksheet |
| Financial Ledger | Basic accounting for treasurers |
| Event Planning | Checklist for community programs |
| Newsletter Template | Communication framework |

### Using Templates
1. **Browse** the template gallery
2. **Preview** by tapping any template card
3. **Open in Editor** to customize
4. **Save Changes** — Your edits are stored locally
5. **Export** as PDF or plain text (coming soon)

### The Editor
- Full-featured text editing
- Section markers guide your input
- Auto-save protects your work
- Reset option restores original template

## Profile — Your Journey

Your personal dashboard for managing saved content and preferences.

### Saved Facts
View all facts you've bookmarked from the Knowledge Base. Tap to revisit, long-press to remove.

### Activity Stats
- Facts saved this month
- WWMD questions asked
- Templates customized
- Days of reflection streak

### Settings
- **Theme**: Light/Dark mode toggle
- **AI / API**: Configure providers for the Garvey Lens—Ollama (local), Open Router / OpenAI-compatible, or Google Gemini. Keys are stored locally only.
- **About**: App version and credits (Whirlwind KB)

## Tips & Best Practices

### For Daily Use
- **Morning Ritual**: Start each day with the Home reflection
- **Bookmark Liberally**: Save anything that resonates; review weekly
- **Search First**: Before asking WWMD, check if the Knowledge Base has facts on your topic

### For Deep Study
- **Follow the Receipts**: Click through to original sources for fuller context
- **Compare Themes**: Use category filters to study one principle deeply
- **Journal Responses**: Copy WWMD wisdom to your own notes

### For Organizing
- **Start with Templates**: Don't reinvent the wheel
- **Customize Thoughtfully**: Adapt language for your community
- **Share the Source**: When presenting ideas, cite Garvey directly

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|:--------|:---------|
| Content not loading | Check internet connection; backend may be restarting |
| WWMD not responding | Backend service may be down; try again in a moment |
| Saved items missing | Data is stored locally; clear cache will reset |
| Dark mode not working | Toggle twice; refresh if needed |
